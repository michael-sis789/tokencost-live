## Short LinkedIn post

Tokencost is a free live calculator for AI agent costs.

It uses current pricing data, models prompt caching and hidden reasoning tokens, and shows monthly cost for multi-turn workflows across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Qwen, and other vendors.

The goal is simple: help builders see what their agent actually costs before they ship.

https://tokencost.live

## Reddit / community post

I built a live AI agent cost simulator that fetches current pricing from `simonw/llm-prices` and estimates multi-turn workflows, cache hit rates, reasoning tokens, and monthly spend.
