Milarepa Video Uploader

Creator-controlled desktop draft publishing for original Buddhist story videos.

Purpose

Milarepa Video Uploader helps an authorized creator send locally stored MP4 videos to their own TikTok account as drafts. It uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

Creator flow

  1. The creator signs in to TikTok and explicitly grants profile and draft-upload access.
  2. The creator selects an original MP4 file stored on their computer.
  3. The uploader sends the file directly to TikTok using the official file-upload endpoint.
  4. TikTok notifies the creator that the draft is ready.
  5. The creator reviews the caption and settings inside TikTok and chooses whether to publish.

Data handling

OAuth tokens are stored only on the creator's computer. Video files are transmitted directly to TikTok and are not retained on our website or a third-party storage service.
